About Food Connect

Food Connect is a real-time platform that helps restaurants and cafés donate surplus food to verified NGOs. We coordinate pickups, map donors and NGOs, and maintain a transparent live feed with photos and timestamps so every donation is traceable and dignified.

According to the UNICEF report, 8.8 lakh children under five years of age lost their life due to starvation in 2018. Over 20 crore Indians sleep empty-stomach every day. More than 7000 Indians die per day due to hunger. As per the reports concluded from the recent data, 25 lakh Indians die every year because of hunger.

Our Mission

Reduce food waste and ensure surplus meals reach vulnerable people through trusted NGO partners.

Our Vision

A connected community where no edible food is wasted and no person goes hungry.

How It Works

Simple steps to get food where it's needed most.

1. Post Donation

Restaurants post food details and pickup address via a simple form.

2. NGO Accepts

Nearby NGOs or volunteers accept the pickup request from the live feed.

3. Delivery & Update

Food is picked up and delivered — the live feed and map update with photos and timestamps.
